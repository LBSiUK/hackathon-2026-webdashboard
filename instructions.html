How to Use Your Dashboard

A simple guide to everything on your screen

🌤️ Weather

The weather panel is split into two halves. The top half shows the weather for the next 24 hours, and the bottom half shows a 2-week forecast.

Each block shows the time or day, the temperature, and a brief weather description (such as "Clear" or "Rain").

If there are more blocks than fit on the screen, you can scroll through them. Tap the circle arrow buttons — the one pointing left goes back, and the one pointing right moves forward.

Tip: You can also swipe left or right directly on the weather panel if you are using a touchscreen.
📰 News Feed

The news panel shows the latest headlines from your chosen news source. Tap any headline to open the full article in your browser.

If you would like to change which news channel the headlines come from, tap the Settings tile in the bottom-right corner of the dashboard, then choose your preferred news source from the list.

Available sources include BBC News, GB News, and Sky News.

Tip: Press the Refresh button at the top of the news panel at any time to load the very latest headlines.
💬 AI Assistant (Chat)

The chat panel on the right-hand side of the screen is your personal AI assistant. You can ask it almost anything, just as you would ask a helpful friend or family member.

Here are some things you can ask it:

• "What is the weather like tomorrow?"
• "How do I copy and paste text on my computer?"
• "Add a doctor's appointment to my calendar for next Tuesday at 10am."
• "What are some easy recipes for dinner tonight?"

Simply click or tap inside the message box at the bottom of the chat panel, type your question, and then press the Send button.

Tip: There are no silly questions — the assistant is very patient and will always try its best to help you.
📅 Calendar & Tasks

The calendar panel shows your events for today, pulled directly from your Google Calendar. If you have signed in with Google (via the Settings page), your appointments and reminders will appear here automatically.

The tasks panel shows your upcoming to-do items from Google Calendar as well.

To add a new event, simply ask the AI assistant in the chat panel — for example: "Add a lunch with Mary on Saturday at 1pm." The assistant will take care of it for you.

If your events are not showing or look out of date, press the Refresh button at the top of the calendar panel to update them.

Tip: To connect your Google Calendar, tap the Settings tile (bottom-right of the dashboard) and tap Sign in with Google. You only need to do this once.
⚙️ Settings

Tap the Settings tile in the bottom-right corner of the dashboard to open the settings page. From here you can:

• Change the background colour theme of the dashboard.
• Choose your preferred news source.
• Sign in with Google to connect your calendar.

When you are done, tap the ✕ button in the top-right corner of the settings window to return to the dashboard.
